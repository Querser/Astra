# Health Analysis and Local Explanation ASTRA

**Rule Version 2.6.** The application works locally and autonomously. Chat by default calls the Ollama installed on this computer via loopback; if it is unavailable, it works according to local rules. Five [main learning profiles](demo-profiles.md) and two additional prediction profiles contain fictional data. Results are not clinical validation.

## Login and Mission Context

You can enter indicators individually or write a complaint in Russian in the «Assistant» chat. `POST /assistant/chat` saves the message, links it to the original complaint, recognizes only explicitly signed numbers, for example «pressure 150/95», then recalculates the analysis and saves the response. `GET /assistant/chat/{crew_id}` shows the history of the selected profile, including previous complaints as archived messages. Within the last 45 minutes of dialogue, the assistant can combine clarifications about one event; a separate candidate catalog still does not combine arbitrary complaints of different dates. Negations such as «snoring is not present» are not considered positive symptoms.

The last explicitly named complaint sets the current topic; a short response like "4 out of 10, nothing else" complements it, and a new topic does not inherit the symptoms of the previous one. Local `qwen3:8b` responds with plain text. The request contains the current utterance, limited history, and materials from [local reference guide](../backend/reference_docs/). For a brief complaint, the assistant clarifies the missing information. Historical measurements are passed as brief summaries. If generation is incomplete, the server repeats the request once. The interface shows the actually used path. Numerical calculations are performed by code, the model interprets them; this division is necessary for reproducible forecasting. For testing without a model, you can set `ASTRA_CHAT_MODEL_ENABLED=0`. The request goes only to loopback, without internet search; external links in the interface are hidden.

The profile stores the mission location, phase, and living module. Reference facts about the ISS, the Moon, and Mars are loaded from [local NASA directory](../backend/reference_docs/ASTRA_MISSION_LOCATIONS.json). Educational measurements of temperature and humidity in the living module are linked to a separate profile. General information about the planet is not a personal radiation dose or air measurement in a specific module. Lunar and Martian bases in the demo are fictional.

## Analysis and Metrics Table

`POST /api/v1/analysis/run` returns structured `parameter_assessments[]` and `integrated_report` along with actual records and references. The table shows the last number, published target if available, medians of early and late points, change, and spread of the seven most recent values. For temperature, the NHS high temperature target of 38 °C is provided. Steps are aligned with personal history: the CDC target of 150 minutes of moderate activity per week and two days of strength training cannot be converted to steps without duration and intensity. Sleep quality and fatigue use explicit internal self-rating 0–10 without fabricated clinical thresholds. Even a single blood pressure measurement receives a terrestrial category AHA/ACC; for hypertension version, repeated values are needed.

[Active catalog](../backend/reference_docs/ASTRA_DEMO_DIAGNOSTIC_CANDIDATES.json) contains five preliminary versions: possible hypoxemia, hypertension, sleep deficit, sleep apnea, and head trauma. For the head trauma version, one original complaint requires a blow to the head and at least one associated symptom; the chat additionally collects messages within a short conversation. Bleeding is not declared intracranial, and trauma is not confirmed without examination. Urgency after a blow with symptoms is based on [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/traumatic-brain-injury/signs-symptoms/index.html) and [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/head-injury-and-concussion/). Ground references are not mission norms.

## Recommendations and conditional scenarios

[Action catalog](../backend/reference_docs/ASTRA_DEMO_GUIDANCE.json) provides specific steps with references: check method and repeat pressure, keep sleep records, clarify complaint, coordinate nutrition and moderate activity with mission plan. In case of signs of head trauma, the chat response shows the order of actions: stop, call someone nearby, contact medical service, apply clean cloth for visible bleeding considering signs of cranial injury, monitor for deterioration. This order relies on [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/head-injury-and-concussion/), [Red Cross](https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/resources/learn-first-aid/head-neck-spinal-injury) and [NWAS](https://www.nwas.nhs.uk/services/emergency-advice/heavy-bleeding/). For confirmed ground hypertension [AHA/ACC](https://professional.heart.org/en/science-news/2025-high-blood-pressure-guideline/top-things-to-know) recommends moderate physical activity, sodium reduction, and medical evaluation; the advice "just move less" is not included in the program. Medication, dosage, cast and other procedures are not prescribed by the program.

The scenarios section shows for each version two **qualitative conditional** paths: what may result from carrying out the steps and what risks are associated with ignoring the problem. Sources and horizons are visible nearby. This is not a calculation of personal treatment effect: there is no data on the person's causal response, treatment method, and mission conditions. The program does not invent numerical magnitude of improvement. A separate statistical forecast of scalar series is not equal to the forecast of disease or treatment.

Numerical forecasting selects one of three methods for each horizon: last value, median of the last seven points, or stable trend of the last ten. The choice is made based on recent past chronological pairs; the last pairs are used for separate mean absolute error check, and when there is sufficient history, earlier pairs define the range of past errors. On the screen, the last eight numbers, selection errors, check error, and comparison with a simple benchmark are visible. A single low SpO₂ remains in the history and requires separate evaluation, although the median does not allow it to shift the forecast of the entire stable series. Two new profiles, added through `python -m app.forecast_demo_seed`, show changes and recovery across all eight numerical indicators.

## Validation and Limitations

An independent validation case in [profile description](demo-profiles.md) sets expected two versions — hypertension and sleep apnea. The test compares them with the actual algorithm response and checks for negations and insufficient history. This is regression validation of rules, not an assessment of sensitivity or specificity on patients. Real annotated data, independent testing, and medical expertise are needed for clinical evaluation.

From the root of the project: `python -m pytest backend/tests -q`; from `frontend/`: `npm run build`. Live validation of two complaints through a temporary database is performed by `python smoke_local_chat.py` from the `backend/` directory. The API and interface continue to work without network and without a local model. The setting `ASTRA_ENABLE_EXPERIMENTAL_LLM=1` relates only to the previous endpoint `/assistant/explain`.
