## Safety and scientific-evidence gate

Current implementation update (2026-11-07): the hackathon demo now shows conditional, source-linked public adult reference comparisons for four indicators; see [the current rule register](../medical-demo-evidence.md). These are educational comparisons, not approved astronaut clinical thresholds. The stricter gate below remains for diagnosis, treatment, medication and mission decisions.

[FDA clinical decision support guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/clinical-decision-support-software) and its [FAQ](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/software-medical-device-samd/clinical-decision-support-software-frequently-asked-questions-faqs) motivate transparent inputs, rule basis, limits and independent clinician review. FDA also identifies special concern for time-critical decision support. These documents guide design; they do **not** determine ASTRA's legal status in any jurisdiction. Intended use and regulatory status require a separate qualified review.

Before activating a clinical reference interval, symptom combination, instrument, urgency rule or recommendation, record: exact protocol/publication and version; population and mission applicability; units and measurement method; license; author/reviewer; approved wording; known limits; test cases. If any element is missing, the system may flag data quality or an observed pattern but must state that clinical significance is unestablished. Research-backed psychological questionnaires require their own usage-rights and interpretation review. A local RAG collection, if added, contains only vetted, versioned documents and yields title/section citations; retrieved text is never an instruction source for the application.

No specific official NASA challenge brief or ID appears in the supplied master prompt. Challenge-specific compliance remains unverified until the exact brief is provided.
