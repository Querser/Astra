# Registry of Sources and Boundaries of Claims

**Status:** `research_only`. Links verified 2026-11-07; the verification date is not the publication date. If no reliably established date is present on the page, it is noted as «not established». The descriptions below are a brief original summary, not a copy of the document or permission to train on its text. Before saving the full text, scales, images, or training corpus, rights are checked separately.

| ID, primary source; date | Population/data and supported claims | Does not confirm; limitations and rights |
|---|---|---|
| `E.CSA.BIO` — [Canadian Space Agency, Bio-Monitor](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/sciences/bio-monitor.asp); updated 2026-02-26 | Research use of wearable device on the ISS; pulse/ECG, blood pressure, breathing, SpO₂, skin temperature, activity during wear | Does not prove continuous use by each crew member, local interface ASTRA, or medical thresholds. We retain the link and our own summary; rights to copy content have not been checked. |
| `E.NASA.EDA` — [NASA Environmental Data App](https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/eda/); date n/a | Historical environmental sensor data from the ISS, including CO₂, temperature, humidity, and radiation context | It is not personal exposure and does not prove a direct telemetry channel to ASTRA. Conditions for loading/reusing a specific dataset are checked separately. |
| `E.NASA.SRAG` — [NASA Space Radiation Analysis Group](https://srag.jsc.nasa.gov/spaceradiation/how/how.cfm); updated 2025-09-24 | Dosimetry of the ISS crew, including Crew Active Dosimeter with timestamps | Does not set individual risk models, ASTRA access to laptop, or rights to transmit personal doses. |
| `E.NASA.RADLAB` — [NASA RadLab](https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/radlab/gui/overview/); date n/a | Series of readings from multiple radiation instruments and their difference by location/type | Does not replace personal dose and does not justify disease prediction. |
| `E.NASA.SANS` — [NASA Human Research Roadmap: SANS](https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=105); page date n/a | Astronauts; functional and structural assessments before, during and after flight, including visual acuity, OCT and ultrasound | A single screen test does not diagnose SANS; frequency/device depends on the study and mission. |
| `E.NASA.STANDARD` — [NASA Standard Measures](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210022783); record 2021 | Repeated standard measurements and questions in space research; useful for distinguishing phases and methods | Does not set ASTRA sampling schedule, personal norm or access to individual results. |
| `E.NASA.EXERCISE` — [NASA, Astronaut Exercise](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/iss-research/astronaut-exercise/); published 2024-05-20 | Equipment and training records of the ISS crew | Duration of load does not measure bone/muscle condition and does not confirm data export to ASTRA. |
| `E.NASA.MASS` — [NASA, SLAMMD activity](https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/spacestation/2020/11/23/advanced-science-in-full-gear-today-as-cosmonauts-relax/); 2020-11-23 | Separate procedure for measuring body mass in microgravity | Does not prove daily measurement or direct input of results into ASTRA. |
| `E.NASA.ACTIGRAPHY` — [NASA station report, Actiwatch](https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/stationreport/2024/02/22/iss-daily-summary-report-2-22-2024/); 2024-02-22 | Use of actigraphy in a specific research episode | Does not prove universal continuous sleep flow or sleep stage by one movement. |
| `E.NASA.CORETEMP` — [NASA station report, Thermo-Mini](https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/stationreport/2021/11/17/iss-daily-summary-report-11-17-2021/); 2021-11-17 | Research assessment of core temperature from thermal flux | Is not equal to direct body temperature and is not a standard ASTRA channel. |
| `E.NASA.CABIN` — [NASA ISS Reference Guide](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/508318main_iss_ref_guide_nov2010.pdf); 2010 | Description of the station's atmosphere systems | Does not establish available ASTRA fields, frequency, exact sensor location or medical threshold for humans. |
| `E.NASA.EVA` — [NASA NTRS, EVA telemetry](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20170009943/downloads/20170009943.pdf); 2017 | Historical example of pulse and CO₂ transmission during EVA on Earth | Does not prove identical sensors in each suit or local ASTRA interface. |
| `E.NASA.SUITSTD` — [NASA Standard 3001, Spacesuits](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/11-0-spacesuits-vol-2/); page date N/A | Requirements for monitoring/suit conditions | The standard requirement does not prove an implemented sensor or access to a specific mission. |
| `E.NASA.CO2` — [NASA CO₂ Technical Brief](https://www.nasa.gov/ochmo-tb-004-carbon-dioxide-2/); page date N/A | Overview of potential CO₂ effects in space environment | Coincidence of headache and cabin series does not prove the cause for a specific individual. |
| `E.FDA.HR` — [FDA, Pulse Oximeter Basics](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/pulse-oximeter-basics); page date N/A | General Earth information about adult resting pulse; supports 60–100 beats/minute | Not a space norm, not a personal baseline series, not a basis for diagnosis. |
| `E.FDA.SPO2` — [FDA, Pulse Oximeter Basics](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/pulse-oximeter-basics); page date N/A | General information about pulse oximetry; typical 95–100% for most healthy people and device limitations | Not an exact universal threshold and not proof of the cause of deviation; requires device/check conditions. |
| `E.NHS.RR` — [Kent Community Health NHS, Dysfunctional Breathing Pattern](https://www.kentcht.nhs.uk/leaflet/dysfunctional-breathing-pattern/); updated 2026-04-26 | General Earth reference 12–20 breaths/minute for adult at rest | Not a norm for workload/flight. The page indicates text reproduction limitations; use only the fact and the link. |
| `E.CDC.SLEEP` — [CDC, About Sleep](https://www.cdc.gov/sleep/about/index.html/); published 2024-05-15 | Recommendation of at least 7 hours of sleep per day for adults 18–60 years | Does not diagnose a sleep disorder and does not determine short-term risk of crew actions. |
| `E.HL7.OBS` — [HL7 FHIR R4 Observation](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/observation-definitions.html) and [Provenance](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/provenance.html); R4, 2019 | Technical reference for method, device, time, origin, and record corrections | Not medical evidence and not a claim of FHIR compatibility with ASTRA; rights to dictionaries/specifications separately. |
| `E.HHS.PRIVACY` — [HHS, De-identification Guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/de-identification/index.html); page date N/A | General reference on risk of re-identification of small groups | Does not claim that U.S. law applies to ASTRA; local legal assessment separately. |

The [availability](availability.md) matrix refers to these original sources. If a specific mission, instrument, or channel changes, the corresponding record must be reviewed with a new date and version, not generalize old evidence.
